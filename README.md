🌟 Overview

Modern hotels thrive on smart decisions.
This Hotel Revenue Management System uses data analytics to decode customer behavior, forecast demand, and maximize revenue through intelligent pricing strategies.

The project explores:

📊 Customer demographics & spending behavior

📈 Occupancy patterns & seasonal demand

💳 Credit score insights & financial eligibility

💰 Dynamic pricing opportunities

🏷️ Booking and cancellation trends

This analytics-driven system empowers hotels to operate smarter, improve cash flow, and deliver unforgettable guest experiences.

🧠 Key Objectives
✅ Maximize hotel revenue using data
✅ Identify high-value customers
✅ Forecast peak demand seasons
✅ Optimize room pricing dynamically
✅ Enhance booking strategies
✅ Improve customer satisfaction & retention
📂 Dataset Highlights

The dataset covers a wide variety of attributes that influence hotel performance.

🔑 Main Feature Categories

💁 Customer Demographics:
Age, gender, income, residence type, customer category

📉 Financial Metrics:
Credit score, loan eligibility, spending score

🏨 Hotel Operations:
Room pricing, occupancy rates, room categories

📅 Demand Patterns:
Seasonal demand, booking frequency, stay duration

🎯 Behavioral Trends:
Preferred room type, repeat visits, customer loyalty indicators

📊 What the Analysis Reveals
🌞 Seasonal Demand

Identify high-demand and low-demand months for strategic pricing.

🛏️ Occupancy-driven Pricing

Adjust room prices based on predicted occupancy levels.

🧍 Customer Segmentation

Group customers by purchasing power and booking preferences.

💳 High-Value Customer Identification

Use credit & spending metrics to target premium guests.

📈 Revenue Optimization

Understand the impact of pricing, behavior, and demographics on hotel income.
